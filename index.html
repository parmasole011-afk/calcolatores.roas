Definitive Guide to ROAS and Profitability in E-commerce

Understanding key metrics to sustain the growth of your advertising campaigns.

What is ROAS and how is it calculated?

ROAS (Return On Ad Spend) is the metric that indicates how much revenue is generated for every dollar invested in advertising campaigns (Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads).

ROAS = Ad Revenue / Ad Spend

Difference between ROAS and ROI

While ROAS solely evaluates the gross return of ad spend ignoring business overhead, ROI (Return On Investment) measures true net profit taking all operational costs into account (product cost, shipping, taxes, and fees).

How to calculate Break-Even ROAS?

Break-Even ROAS represents the minimum threshold beyond which ad campaigns begin to yield real profit. Below this value, every sale generated results in a loss for the store.

BE ROAS = Net Price / Gross Margin

5 Practical Levers to Reduce Breakeven ROAS

Optimize profitability without increasing ad spend:

1. COGS Renegotiation

Once you reach 50 orders/month, ask your supplier for a 5-10% cut on unit cost or combined shipping.

2. Packaging Optimization

Reduce weight and dimensions to fall into lower courier price brackets (e.g., under 500g).

3. Return Prevention

Add accurate size charts, unretouched real photos, and videos to eliminate returns caused by mismatched expectations.

4. Payment Gateway Fees

Disable high-fee, low-conversion payment methods and use gateways with tier-based pricing.

5. Micro Price Increases

A 3-5% list price increase goes straight to net profit, significantly lowering the required breakeven ROAS.
